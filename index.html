The aesthetic

Dark florals, heirloom thread

Our pieces take their cues from pressed-flower albums, faded seed catalogs and the velvet-lined sewing boxes of another century. Each one begins on undyed cotton canvas and is finished in the colors of an old botanical plate, with garnet and plum blooms, forest-green leaves and a single line of antique gold. The result is quiet and a little moody, made to be carried every day and kept for years.

How custom orders work

Every piece is made for one person, and nothing is stitched until you have approved the design.

  1. I

    By form or email

    Send an inquiry

    Use the form below to tell us which piece you would like, the words or initials, and the thread colors you love.

  2. II

    Proof and quote

    Approve your proof

    We reply with a stitch proof showing your lettering, colors and placement, along with a quote. Ask for changes until it is right.

  3. III

    2–3 weeks

    Stitched and sent

    Once you approve, we embroider your piece to order, wrap it by hand and ship it to you.
